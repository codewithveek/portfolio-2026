Cutroom starts by **reading the repository**, redacting secrets before anything else sees the code.

It writes a **product brief** in which every feature cites file-and-line evidence, then a **storyboard**. Before rendering, it **audits every claim** against the code: UI labels, code excerpts, numbers and commands, and finally a Gemma judge.

A person approves the cut. Then **Remotion** renders the video and the audio is normalised to −16 LUFS.

In **local mode** the whole pipeline runs offline, with Gemma for reasoning and Kokoro for the voice. A privacy guard and an egress guard are enforced in code, not in documentation.

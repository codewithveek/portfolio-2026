Zia is one product with two builds. The **Node build** (`server/` and `client/`) and the **Cloudflare Workers build** (`cloudflare/api` and `cloudflare/web`) share TiDB and the same feature set, and every change lands in both.

Authentication runs on better-auth: organisations, two-factor authentication, admin tooling and API keys. Plans (Starter, Growth, Scale) set seat limits, and an organisation that is deleted stays restorable for 30 days.

The **Zia AI assistant** sits on a model-agnostic harness. Merchants get monthly AI credits, and any action that changes data waits for the merchant to confirm it.

The **staff console** runs on its own Worker with separate staff accounts, required 2FA, owner/admin/support/analyst roles and an audit log of every action.
